Miranda Warning

FOP Lodge 7 Handbook 2024, page 164

REQUIRED WARNINGS TO BE GIVEN PRIOR TO INTERROGATION

1. Before we ask you any question, it is our duty to advise you of your rights.

2. You have a right to remain silent.

3. Anything you say can and may be used against you in a court or other proceedings.

4. Do you understand that you have a right to talk to a lawyer before we ask you questions, and to have him with you during question?

5. If you cannot afford or otherwise obtain a lawyer and you want one, a lawyer will be appointed for you, and we will not ask any questions until s/he has been appointed.

6. If you decide to answer now with or without a lawyer, you still have the right to stop questioning at any time, or to stop the questioning for the purpose of consulting a lawyer.

7. You may waive the right to advice of counsel and your right to remain silent, and you may answer questions or make statement without consulting a lawyer if you so desire.

8. Do you understand each of these rights?

9. Do you wish to answer questions at this time?

(*Juvenile Miranda Warning step 2-6: After reading above department member musk ask: 1. Do you want to have a lawyer? 2. Do you want to talk to me.)
